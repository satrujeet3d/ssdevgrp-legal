Privacy Policy

Image & Document Toolkit
Developed by SSDev.grp
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Image & Document Toolkit handles information when you use the Android app.

Information the app uses

The app uses the image and PDF files you choose to import, capture, convert, or otherwise process. It also stores local library information, such as file names and creation details, so it can display and manage files in the app.

The app does not require you to create an account.

How files are processed

Image and PDF processing is performed on your device. SSDev.grp does not operate a server that receives or stores copies of your files.

When you select a file using Android’s file or photo picker, the app accesses the file you selected. If you choose to take pictures, the app uses the camera for that action.

Storage and file handling

Files you import or create are stored in app-managed storage on your device so the app can list and process them. SSDev.grp does not keep server-side copies of those files.

If you export a file to your Gallery or Downloads, Android saves a separate copy at the destination you selected. That copy is managed separately from the app’s library.

Sharing and external services

The app does not send your files to SSDev.grp. If you choose to export a file or open a PDF using another app, Android passes the file to the destination you selected. That app’s own privacy practices then apply.

Android’s system picker may show files from services connected to your device. If you choose a file from such a service, that provider’s terms and privacy practices may also apply.

Advertising and analytics

The app version covered by this policy does not include an advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDK. If these services are added in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe their data practices before that version is released.

Permissions

The app uses camera access when you choose to take pictures. Android’s file picker provides access to files you select. Storage-related access may be used for export features on older Android versions.

Data retention and deletion

Imported and created files are stored locally on your device for use by the app. SSDev.grp does not retain copies of those files on its servers. Exported copies are saved separately in the destination you choose.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ssdevgrp.appsupport@gmail.com .
