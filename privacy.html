Privacy Policy

Image & Document Toolkit
Developed by SSDev.grp
Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image & Document Toolkit handles information when you use the Android app.

Information the app uses

The app uses images and PDF files that you choose to import, capture, convert, or process. It also stores local library information, including file names and related metadata, so it can list and manage your files.

The app does not require you to create an account.

How files are processed

The app’s image and PDF tools process files on your device. The app does not provide cloud storage or backup to SSDev.grp servers, and the inspected app code contains no feature that uploads My Files content to SSDev.grp.

When you choose a file through Android’s system picker, the app accesses the file you selected. The picker may show files supplied by other apps or services on your device; their own privacy practices apply.

Storage and exports

Imported files and completed outputs are stored in the app’s private My Files storage on your device. The app also stores library metadata, such as file names, in a local database.

When you export an image, the app creates a separate copy in Pictures/ImageDocumentToolkit, which may appear in Gallery. When you export a PDF, the app creates a separate copy in Downloads/ImageDocumentToolkit. These exported copies are separate from the files in My Files.

The app does not delete completed My Files items or exported files as part of normal processing or routine cleanup. Temporary files used during an unfinished save, crop, camera session, or PDF preview may be cleaned up by the app.

Deleting files and app data

The current version does not provide an in-app option to delete individual My Files items or clear the My Files library.

On an Android 15 test device, using Android Settings to clear this app’s storage removed the app-private My Files files and library database. A previously exported Gallery image remained on the device after that action.

The effect of uninstalling the app, and the effect of clearing storage on PDF files exported to Downloads, were not verified in this test. Exported copies are separate from My Files; use your device’s Gallery or Files app to manage them.

Permissions

The app requests camera access when you choose to take pictures for Create PDF. The camera capture flow is configured without audio recording, and the app does not request microphone access.

On Android 9 and older, the app may request storage-write access when you choose to export a file to Gallery or Downloads. Selecting existing images or PDFs uses Android’s system file picker.

The inspected merged Android release package also lists RECORD_AUDIO and READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE declarations contributed or implied by the camera library. The app does not request microphone access, does not record audio in its camera flow, and does not use READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE to browse your photo library. These declarations may change if the Android configuration is updated before release.

Advertising and analytics

The current app version does not include advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs and does not show ads. If these services are added in a future version, this Policy will be updated to describe the applicable data practices before that version is released.

Sharing with other apps

The app does not share your files with other apps in the background. If you choose to export a file or open a PDF with another app, Android provides that app access for the action you requested. That app’s own terms and privacy practices then apply.

Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated if the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version and its update date will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact ssdevgrp.appsupport@gmail.com .
