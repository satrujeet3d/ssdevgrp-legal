Privacy Policy

Image & Document Toolkit

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Who we are

Image & Document Toolkit is published by SSDev.grp. For privacy questions, contact us at ssdevgrp.appsupport@gmail.com.

Information and files you use in the app

The app processes images and PDFs that you choose or create using its features. This can include image import, compression, resizing, cropping, batch processing, PDF creation, PDF-to-image conversion, and camera capture for a PDF.

Imported files and files created by the app are stored in the app’s private “My Files” area, together with library information needed to show those files in the app. The app does not upload these files to an SSDev.grp server.

You can export a separate copy of an image to Gallery or a PDF to Downloads. Exported copies are separate from the app’s private My Files copies. On Android 15 (API 35), a Gallery export remained on the device after Android’s Clear storage action removed the app’s private files. Behavior on other Android versions has not been verified here.

Permissions and file selection

  • Camera: The app may request Camera permission when you choose Take pictures in Create PDF. It uses the camera for still-image capture; the app does not record audio.
  • Storage write on Android 9 and older: The app may request write access only when you save an export copy to Gallery or Downloads. Newer Android versions use Android’s MediaStore for these exports without that permission.
  • Choosing existing files: The app uses Android’s system file picker to let you select images or PDFs. It does not request broad permission to read your shared photos or files.

Temporary files and saved files

Temporary files may be created while a save, crop, camera capture, or PDF preview is in progress. The app may clean up those temporary files. It does not provide an in-app action to delete or clear completed items in My Files, and normal app use does not automatically delete completed My Files items or finished Gallery/Downloads exports.

Android system actions are separate from the app. For example, Clear storage removed the app’s private files and library database in the API 35 check described above. You can remove exported copies separately with your device’s Gallery or Files app. Uninstall behavior has not been verified here.

Ads, analytics, and other services

This policy describes the current app build. At the time this version was reviewed, it did not include an advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash reporting SDK. If these services are added, this policy and the relevant Play Console disclosures will be updated before release.

Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose image and document utility and is not designed specifically to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with its updated date.
