Last updated: 11 November 2026

Contact

Files and on-device processing

Image & Document Toolkit lets you choose or create images and PDFs for features such as compression, resizing, cropping, batch processing, PDF creation, PDF-to-image conversion, and camera capture for a PDF.

Files imported into or created by the app are stored in the app’s private “My Files” area, with local library information used to display them in the app. The app does not upload your image or PDF contents to an SSDev.grp server.

The app does not attach your image or PDF contents to its Google Mobile Ads requests. Ads are displayed in the app’s browsing areas, such as Tools and My Files, and not in the image or PDF processing flows.

You may export a separate image copy to Gallery or a PDF copy to Downloads. These exported copies are separate from the app’s private My Files copies. On Android 15 (API 35), a Gallery export remained on the device after Android’s Clear storage action removed the app’s private files and library database. This behavior has not been verified on every Android version.

Permissions and selecting files

  • Camera: The app may request Camera permission when you choose Take pictures in Create PDF. The feature captures still photos; the app does not record audio.
  • Storage write on Android 9 and older: The app may request write access when exporting a copy to Gallery or Downloads on those Android versions. Newer Android versions use MediaStore for these exports without that permission.
  • Choosing existing files: The app uses Android’s system file picker to let you select images or PDFs. It does not request broad permission to read shared photos or files.

Advertising and information processed by Google

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. The SDK may automatically collect and share information with Google for ad delivery, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service performance. Depending on the device and configuration, this information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device’s general location;
  • interactions with the app, such as app launches, taps, and ad or video views;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the app or SDK; and
  • device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, where available and applicable.

Google states that information collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Google’s processing is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy and applicable Google advertising terms. The app does not control Google’s data-retention practices.

Advertising choices and consent requirements can depend on your location, device settings, and the advertising configuration. Before the app serves live ads, its consent and privacy-choice features will be configured for the applicable requirements. You can also review advertising controls in your Android device settings.

Temporary files and completed files

Temporary files may be created while a save, crop, camera capture, or PDF preview is in progress. The app may clean up these temporary files. The app does not provide an in-app action to delete or clear completed My Files items, and normal use does not automatically delete completed My Files items or finished Gallery or Downloads exports.

Android system actions are separate from the app. In the Android 15 (API 35) check described above, Clear storage removed the app’s private files and library database, while the tested Gallery export remained. Uninstall behavior and Downloads-export survival after Clear storage have not been verified. You can remove exported copies with your device’s Gallery or Files app.

Other services

The current app build does not include a separate analytics or crash reporting SDK. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process app interactions and diagnostic information as described in this policy.

Children

Image & Document Toolkit is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children. The app’s audience and advertising settings should be configured consistently with its intended audience and applicable requirements.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app’s features, advertising services, or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
